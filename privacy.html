Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Welcome to HopeHub. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website.

1. Information We Collect

HopeHub may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using certain website features.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Messages submitted through the contact form
  • Information provided through newsletter forms

2. Information Stored in Your Browser

Some HopeHub features may use your browser's local storage to remember preferences and website activity.

This may include:

  • Favorite videos
  • Liked videos
  • Theme preferences
  • Other website preferences

This information is stored locally in your browser and is not automatically sent to HopeHub's server.

3. Login and Signup Information

HopeHub may provide optional login and signup features. Information entered into these features may be stored locally in your browser depending on how the website functionality is implemented.

Do not enter sensitive personal information, passwords used for other services, financial information, or other confidential information into HopeHub.

4. Contact Forms

If you contact HopeHub through a contact form, the information you provide may be used to respond to your message or request.

5. Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to a HopeHub newsletter, the email address you provide may be used for newsletter-related communications.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

HopeHub may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or support advertising and analytics services.

7. Third-Party Services

HopeHub may use third-party services such as YouTube, Google services, analytics providers, advertising networks, or other external services.

These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. YouTube Videos

HopeHub may embed or link to videos hosted by YouTube. When you interact with embedded YouTube content, YouTube may collect information according to Google's and YouTube's own policies.

9. Children's Privacy

HopeHub is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the website. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. External Links

HopeHub may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as HopeHub develops new features or services. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled on HopeHub, please contact us through the contact section of our website.
